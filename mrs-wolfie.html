ABOUT MRS WOLFIE

She started boxing to survive her past — to fight through the shadows of domestic violence and rebuild the parts of herself that had been taken from her.

Boxing became more than a sport. It became her lifeline.

With every round, her confidence grew. With every punch, she felt herself coming back. With Chris “Mr Wolfie” by her side, Zoey discovered a strength within herself that could no longer be taken away.

Together, Zoey and Chris co-founded Team Wolfpack, building something driven by strength, unity and purpose.
