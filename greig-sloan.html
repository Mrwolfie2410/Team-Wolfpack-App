ABOUT GREIG SLOAN

He started boxing to survive his past — to fight through the shadows of hardship and rebuild the parts of himself that had been broken.

Boxing became more than a sport. It became his lifeline.

With every round, his confidence grew. With every punch, he felt himself coming back.

With the people by his side and boxing in his corner, Greig discovered a strength within himself that could no longer be taken away.

Today, Greig proudly represents his own team and is a proud member of Team Wolfpack.

Team Wolfpack is built on strength, loyalty and unity, and was solely created by Co-Founders Chris “Mr Wolfie” and Zoey “Mrs Wolfie”.

Today, Greig’s mission is bigger than his own journey.

He wants to encourage others to step into the ring — not simply to fight, but to rise. To discover their confidence, their strength and what they are capable of becoming.

His message is simple: No matter what you have been through, and no matter what you are facing today, your past does not have to define your future.
